📦 How to Install

  • Download all 3 files using the download tools on the right: manifest.json, popup.html, and automation.js.
  • Place all three downloaded files together inside a single, dedicated folder on your computer named AmazonAutomator.
  • Open Google Chrome and type chrome://extensions/ directly into your URL address bar.
  • In the upper-right corner of the Extensions page, toggle the switch that says Developer mode to ON.
  • In the upper-left corner, click the button that says Load unpacked.
  • Select your AmazonAutomator folder. The extension is now installed and accessible from your browser extension bar!
